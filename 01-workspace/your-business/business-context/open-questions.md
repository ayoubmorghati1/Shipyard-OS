## When To Add A Question

Add a question when:

- the agent cannot confidently build a Work System without more context
- source material contradicts existing context
- an offer, audience, rule, or workflow is unclear
- a repeated correction suggests missing business memory

## When To Close A Question

Close a question when:

1. The answer is confirmed.
2. The relevant business-context page is updated.
3. The source is recorded in `source-log.md`.
4. Any decision is logged in `../decisions/decision-log.md` if needed.
