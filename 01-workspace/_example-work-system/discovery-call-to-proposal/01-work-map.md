# Work Map - Discovery Call To Proposal

## Business Goal

Increase sales follow-through by sending useful proposals within 24 hours of qualified discovery calls.

## Workflow Name

Discovery call to proposal.

## Current Trigger

A prospect completes a discovery call.

## Current Steps

1. Call happens.
2. Notes or transcript are saved somewhere.
3. Founder reviews the call.
4. Founder decides what to offer.
5. Proposal is drafted.
6. Follow-up email is drafted.
7. Proposal is sent.
8. Details are logged in the CRM or project tracker.

## People Involved

Founder, sales lead, delivery lead, prospect.

## Tools Involved

Call recorder, notes, offer documents, proposal examples, CRM, email.

## Knowledge Sources

Offer structure, pricing logic, case studies, proposal examples, brand voice, common objections.

## Friction Points

- Proposals take too long after calls.
- The founder is needed for every proposal.
- Follow-up quality varies.
- Important details from the call get missed.

## AI Opportunity

Use AI to summarize the call, extract needs, match the offer, draft the proposal, and draft the follow-up email for human review.

## Risk Level

Medium.

Risk comes from overpromising scope, misreading commercial details, or sending unreviewed client communication.

## Success Metric

Proposal sent within 24 hours of qualified discovery call.
